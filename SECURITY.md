# Security policy

## Reporting

Do not open a public issue for a vulnerability, exposed credential, or privacy-sensitive finding. Use GitHub's private vulnerability reporting for this repository.

Include the affected revision, impact, reproduction steps, and the smallest safe evidence needed to investigate. Do not attach real photos, Photos databases, thumbnails, OCR output, asset identifiers, precise coordinates, account details, credentials, or user-specific audit reports.

## Scope

Security reports may cover unsafe filesystem or Photos-library access, authorization bypasses, privacy leaks, destructive defaults, secret exposure, or validation gaps in the published repository. General support questions and organization advice belong in ordinary issues when they contain no private data.

The supported development line is the current `main` branch. A report is not resolved until the fix and relevant regression checks pass; publication, installation, and release remain separate decisions.
